October 1984

On the night of a terrible storm, Ilya the postman carried his last bag of letters across the bridge and never came home. Forty years later his ghost rises every night by the old mailbox: until the bag is empty, he cannot rest.

Keep to the shadows. Lamps, windows and searchlights reveal a ghost, and cats and dogs can sense him even in the dark.

A candle is lit in the window. The letter was forty years late, but someone was waiting.
